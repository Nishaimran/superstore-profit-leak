# superstore-profit-leak

**Status:** Cleaning + Excel analysis

**Grain:** One row represents one product line/item within an order.

## 4 Business Questions

1. Which products make sales but lose money?
2. Do high discounts tend to be associated with lower profit?
3. Which segment has high sales but weak profit margin?
4. What happens to profit when discounts are above 20%?

## This Week I Answered

* Q1: Which products make sales but lose money?
* Q2: Do high discounts tend to be associated with lower profit?

## Top Finding

High-discount orders had **-$135,376.06 total profit** and a **-37.32% profit margin**. This shows an association between high discounts and losses, but does not prove that high discounts caused the losses.

The top loss-making product was **Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print**, with **$11,099.963 in sales** and a **-$8,879.9704 loss**.
